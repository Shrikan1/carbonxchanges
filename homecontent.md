   ▼
🛰️ Agent Verification
   │
   ▼
✅ Admin Approval

   ▼
🔗 ERC-1155 Token Minted
   │
   ▼
🛒 Listed on Marketplace
   │
   ▼
💰 Buyer Purchases
   │
   ▼
🔥 Credits Burned (Retired)
   │
   ▼
📜 Retirement Certificate Generated

This section instantly demonstrates the **end-to-end value of your platform** and highlights the blockchain integration, making your project stand out during demos and evaluations.
